Frequently asked

Where does transcription happen? Your audio is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI provider, Google (Gemini), transcribed, and not retained by them or by us. You are asked to agree in the app before anything is sent. Your original recordings are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the iOS Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Notably → Cancel. You can also cancel from inside the App Store.

How do I delete my data? In the app, go to Settings → Delete all data. This permanently removes all recordings, transcripts, notes, and audio from your device.
