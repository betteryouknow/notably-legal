Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 27, 2026

Notably (“we”, “us”, the “App”) is built privacy-first. This policy explains exactly what data the App handles and what it does not. In plain terms: your recordings and transcription stay on your device, and we never sell your data.

1. On-device recording & transcription

  • Audio never leaves your device. Recording and speech-to-text transcription run entirely on your device using Apple’s on-device Speech framework.
  • Audio files, transcripts, notes, action items, and chat history are stored locally on your device only. We do not have a copy.
  • You can delete any recording, or all data, at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

2. AI notes & chat (transcript text only)

When you choose to generate AI notes (summary, key points, decisions, action items) or use “ask this meeting” chat, the App sends the transcript text (not your audio) to our processing service, which forwards it to a third-party large-language-model provider to produce the result. We:

  • send only the transcript text needed to produce your notes or answer;
  • do not retain transcript text on our servers after the request is processed (it is not logged or stored);
  • never send your audio, your contacts, or your identity with these requests.

If you never use AI notes or chat, no transcript text is ever transmitted off your device.

3. Permissions we request

  • Microphone — to record meetings, lectures, and voice memos.
  • Speech Recognition — to transcribe your recordings on your device.
  • Reminders (optional) — only if you choose to turn an action item into a system reminder.

You can change these in the iOS Settings app at any time.

4. Subscriptions & purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated subscription status so the App can unlock premium features. See Apple’s privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not use your recordings, transcripts, or notes to train AI models.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts to use the App.

6. Diagnostics

Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and usage diagnostics if you opt in to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings. This data is not linked to your identity and contains no recording or transcript content.

7. Children

Notably is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Your rights

Because your content lives on your device, you are in control: you can view, edit, export, or permanently delete it at any time within the App. To exercise any data request or ask a question, contact us below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
